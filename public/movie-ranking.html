The Best Movies According to Me

My Top 3 Movies of all time -

The Forbidden Kingdom

Jackie Chan and Jet Li star in this rousing adventure about a martial arts movie fan who finds a mystical staff that transports him to ancient China.

The Sorcerer's Apprentice

Master sorcerer Balthazar hires Dave, an average guy, as an apprentice to aid him in saving the city from his arch-enemy, Maxim Horvath. Thus, Dave gets a crash course in the art and science of magic.

Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time

With the help of a special, magical dagger that can help reverse time, a young prince and a princess must stop an evil villain whose actions threaten to destroy the world.
